# Datasets and licensing

No dataset is redistributed in this repository. Each entry below gives the
source and the terms under which it was used.

| dataset | role | source | terms |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kodak-24 | INR fitting: tuning subset (8 images) and held-out evaluation subset (16 images) | https://r0k.us/graphics/kodak/ | Released by Eastman Kodak for unrestricted research use. Place `kodim01.png` ... `kodim24.png` in `data/kodak/`. |
| DIV2K (validation split) | confirmatory INR evaluation, first 16 images by filename, resized to 768x512 | https://data.vision.ee.ethz.ch/cvl/DIV2K/DIV2K_valid_HR.zip | Agustsson & Timofte, NTIRE 2017. Academic research use. Fetched and preprocessed by `experiments/prepare_div2k.py`; not redistributed here. |
| `camera`, `astronaut` | INR fitting on small images, used for the mechanism study | bundled with scikit-image | Public domain / permissive; see scikit-image documentation. |
| California Housing, Concrete Compressive Strength, Energy Efficiency | full-batch regression suite | scikit-learn and UCI Machine Learning Repository | Public research datasets, downloaded on demand into `data/`. |
| synthetic coupled quadratics | applicability map and detector ground truth | generated by `experiments/diag_coupling.py` | Generated at run time from a fixed seed; nothing to download. |

`data/` is git-ignored. Every acquisition step is scripted, so the data state
can be rebuilt from this repository alone.
